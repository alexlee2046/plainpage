素页 Plainpage

Safari 去广告 · Safari Ad Blocker for iPhone

素页用两层方式去掉 Safari 里的广告：约 8.6 万条规则由 Safari 在系统层直接拦截广告与弹窗；规则漏掉的广告，再由本地 AI 模型识别。本地 AI 由 Jev 模型训练，学习了近 5 万个真实网页元素。所有判断都在你的 iPhone 上完成，不上传任何浏览内容。

Plainpage removes ads in Safari in two layers: about 86,000 rules let Safari block ads and pop-ups at the system level, and an on-device AI model catches ads the rules miss. The on-device AI was trained with the Jev model on nearly 50,000 real web page elements. Everything runs on your iPhone; nothing you browse is uploaded.
