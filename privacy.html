隐私政策

适用于 iOS App「素页 Plainpage」及其 Safari 扩展 · 生效日期 2026-09-24

我们不收集任何数据

素页不收集、不存储、不上传、不出售任何个人信息或使用数据。App 没有账号系统，不含任何统计、广告或追踪 SDK。

广告判断全部在设备上完成

「素页 规则拦截」把拦截规则交给 Safari，由 Safari 在系统内部匹配，素页看不到你访问的网址。「素页 AI 过滤」在网页内运行本地模型判断页面元素是否为广告，判断过程和结果只存在于你的设备上，不会发送到任何服务器。

网页扩展权限

AI 过滤需要你在 Safari 设置中允许其访问网站，这是在页面内运行本地模型、移除广告元素所必需的。扩展不会读取或传输表单内容、密码、Cookie 或浏览记录。

联网情况

素页本身不发起网络请求。更新拦截规则和模型随 App 版本通过 App Store 下发。

儿童隐私

素页不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

变更与联系

本政策如有变更，会更新本页并修改生效日期。问题请见 技术支持。

Privacy Policy

For the iOS app "Plainpage" and its Safari extensions · Effective 2026-09-24

We collect no data

Plainpage does not collect, store, upload, or sell any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts and no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Ad detection happens on your device

"Plainpage Blocker" hands its rules to Safari, which matches them internally; Plainpage never sees the addresses you visit. "Plainpage AI Filter" runs an on-device model inside web pages to decide whether page elements are ads. The process and its results stay on your device and are never sent to any server.

Web extension permission

The AI Filter needs your permission in Safari settings to access websites, which is required to run the on-device model in the page and remove ad elements. It does not read or transmit form contents, passwords, cookies, or browsing history.

Network

Plainpage itself makes no network requests. Rule and model updates ship with app updates through the App Store.

Children

Plainpage collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated. For questions see Support.
